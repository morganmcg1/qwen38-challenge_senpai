READING
  The `carry` column is the physically correct accounting: when the
  adaptive rule offers a depth shorter than the latent run, the run
  remainder is not destroyed, it is accepted on the next round.  With
  that accounting the loop manufactures pooled rho1 = 0.0980 out of a
  provably exchangeable input, against 0.1168 observed on the real
  adaptive leg, i.e. 84% of the observed lag-1 autocorrelation.  It
  manufactures tracker correlation 0.1725 against 0.1662 observed,
  i.e. all of it.
  benchfixture is the one prompt where the observed value is far above
  the manufactured value, and it is also the one prompt that is 79%
  truncated at depth 7, so its fixed-depth-7 input is itself censored
  and the control cannot be trusted there.
  Conclusion: the apparent within-prompt serial structure on the
  adaptive arm is an artefact of the rule's own depth feedback plus
  censoring.  It is not exploitable signal.  The open-loop p7 arm,
  which has ~1% censoring, is the arm that carries the real answer,
  and there rho1 = +0.0014.
