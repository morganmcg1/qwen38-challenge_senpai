Reconciling E115's f = 0.667 with Finding 22
==============================================================================
E115 identity:  A_round = f * A_tensor + (1 - f)
  A_round  = 2 * (1 - 0.18) = 1.640   RATE ratio
  A_tensor = 1.960                  TIME ratio, E115 b_msplit
  f        = 0.667

The two inputs are not the same kind of object. A rate ratio rises
towards G as grouping improves; a time ratio falls towards 1. As a
rate ratio the b_msplit result is 2 / 1.96 = 1.0204, and
  f = (1.640 - 1) / (1.0204 - 1) = 31.4, which is impossible for a fraction.

Redo it in one consistent TIME frame. With phi the share of the
one-group round that scales with the group count:
  t2 / t1 = (1 - phi) + phi * (q2 / q1)
  t2 / t1 = 1 / (1 - 0.18) = 1.2195   E100
  q2/q1 = 1.7336 ([4+4]/[4])  ->  phi = 0.299
  q2/q1 = 1.9510 ([3+3]/[3])  ->  phi = 0.231

Finding 22 wide-branch qualifying share  0.600
Finding 22 whole streaming subtotal      0.898

phi lands well below both, so E100's collapse cannot be read as
'this fraction of the round is group-scaling matvec work'. The two
measurements describe different partitions: E100 compared the
PRE-E100 [3+2] map against the current [5] map, and [3+2] is two
groups of 3 and 2 rows, which are intrinsically cheaper per group
than the [4+4] the current tree runs at M = 8.

Direct check of E100 in the M frame, mlp.gate_up:
  [5] measured                 664.87 us
  [3] + [2] isolated           891.21 us
  grouping saving from [3+3]   2.45 %

  [5] faster than [3+2] by     23.52 %
  x wide-branch share 0.600   -> 14.11 % of the round
  E100 measured round collapse  18.0 %

The wide-branch tensors alone explain most of E100's 18.0 %, and
the narrow-branch out_proj and down_proj family, 28.666 % of the
round, changed partition in the same commit. Finding 22 and E100
agree. E115's f = 0.667 does not follow from either and should be
withdrawn.

==============================================================================
Frame dilution: every campaign A is an upper bound, and 1.640 is
not a diluted current-tree kernel constant
==============================================================================
A_local is a ratio of ROUND times. With round time t = F + q and any
non-QMV F > 0:
  A_round = 2 (F + q1) / (F + q2)  >  A_kernel = 2 q1 / q2

Solving A_round = 1.640 for A_kernel at fixed F:
     F   A_kernel
  0.00      1.640
  0.10      1.600
  0.20      1.550
  0.30      1.486
  0.40      1.400

A_kernel is highest at F = 0 and falls as F rises, so 1.640 is the
ceiling this model allows. Now measure A_kernel directly on the
shipped partitions, mlp.gate_up, as a rate ratio 2 t1 / t2:
      [4+4] vs [4]  A_kernel = 1.1537
      [3+3] vs [3]  A_kernel = 1.0251
    [3+3+3] vs [3]  A_kernel = 1.0713

Every measured A_kernel is far below 1.640, so the dilution model
is consistent in sign: the round ratio does exceed the kernel ratio.
The bracket in the E115 pre-registration, A_kernel = 1.550 at F = 20 %
and 1.400 at F = 40 %, is therefore an upper bound and a loose one.
The directly measured current-tree A_kernel at the width that
carries 84 % of rounds is 1.154. A [4+4] group returns 15 % more
logical bytes per second than a [4] group, not the 40 to 55 % the
bracket allows, so the bracket cannot be used as an estimate.
