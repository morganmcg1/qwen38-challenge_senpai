  (3) THE SIGNAL IS REAL; THE LEVER IS NOT. Decompose the variance
      of the shipped rule's drafting depth (cap 8) into between-

      the between part.

      So between-prompt structure EXISTS, and the observable
      separates the prompts almost perfectly (misclassification
      below 1e-7 by r = 16). This experiment does not fail from
      weak signal or from sampling noise. It fails because the CAP
      has no per-prompt optimum to find: it is inert where the
      prompts differ most, and maximal wherever it binds. The
      remaining within-prompt variation is already handled by the
      shipped per-round greedy price, which is a finer instrument
      than any per-prompt constant.
