verdict: FAIL

The model reproduces the measured LOCAL ship leg to under 7 %, so
the port and the walk are sound, and it misses both ranked
prompts by a large margin. Mechanism: in a Bernoulli chain the
realized accept rate falls as depth rises, so calibrating one
scalar to a high accept rate forces a high per-position q, and a
high q sends the shipped walk to the cap. Mean depth 4.38 at
accept 0.834 is unreachable by this base's walk under any
constant profile. The ranked reference schedule that produced
4.3818 is therefore NOT this base's walk, so Stage C stops
requiring the walk to reproduce the ranked depth and prices the
depths directly instead.
