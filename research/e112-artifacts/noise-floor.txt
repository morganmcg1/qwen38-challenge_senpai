A cut that far into the tail truncates a negligible share of the same-mode
population, so `D same` is a usable resolution and not an artefact of the cut.

Caveat on the DRAFT columns. The same-mode filter is a cut on the DRAFT
probe itself, so `D same` is conditioned on its own value. The separation
check above bounds that bias. `D all` mixes in the FACT-2 mode flip, which is
a real run-level state rather than measurement noise.
